### Suggestions for Music, Transitions, Effects, Audio, and More in CapCut
Since you're using CapCut on your Pixel 3 (great for mobile editing—it's free and beginner-friendly), and Windscribe VPN (use it to access any region-locked music/effects if needed, e.g., set to US for more options), here's tailored advice. #### 1. **Music**
   - **Style Recommendation**: Upbeat electronic/hip-hop beats with a fast tempo (120-140 BPM) to build excitement—think motivational tech tutorial soundtracks. Avoid lyrics to not clash with your voiceover; go instrumental.
   - **Specific CapCut Suggestions** (Search in CapCut's "Music" library under "Free" or "Trending"):
     - "Tech Hustle" or similar: Look for "Electronic Motivational" or "Future Bass" tracks like "Energy Boost" or "Rapid Fire Beat" (free options).
     - "Cyber Pulse": A synth-heavy track for that "hacker" feel—start low-energy for hook, build to drops at "Boom" moments.
     - Alternatives: "Quick Wins" (upbeat pop-electronic) or import a free royalty-free track from YouTube Audio Library via Windscribe if CapCut's library feels limited (download to phone, then import).
   - **How to Use**: Add music to the entire timeline. Fade in/out at start/end. Volume: Set to 30-50% under voiceover. Sync beats to transitions (e.g., drop on "Yes, absolutely!").
   - **Timing Tip**: Use CapCut's beat detection—tap "Auto Beat" when adding music to auto-sync clips.

#### 2. **Transitions**
   - **Style**: Keep them quick and dynamic to maintain pace—Reels under 60 sec need snappy flow to hold attention.
   - **Specific CapCut Suggestions** (In "Transitions" tab, search by name):
     - **Zoom In/Out**: For hooking into steps (e.g., zoom on dashboard). Use "Zoom Blur" or "Dynamic Zoom" between clips.
     - **Swipe or Glitch**: "Glitch Transition" for techy feel when jumping to terminal clips (e.g., from dashboard to SSH key gen).
     - **Fade/Cut**: Simple "Quick Cut" for fast pacing, or "Fade to Black" for subtle shifts. Avoid overusing—2-3 per Reel max.
     - **Face Cam Pops**: Use "Pop In" transition (under "Effects > Animation") for your 2-sec face appearances—make it slide in from side.

#### 3. **Effects and Visuals**
